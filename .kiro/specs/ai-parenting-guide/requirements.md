# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This document outlines the requirements for the "Raising AI" educational platform - a web and mobile application based on De Kai's book "Raising AI: An Essential Guide to Parenting Our Future." The platform will provide interactive learning experiences, community discussions, and practical tools to help users understand and practice ethical AI development, treating AI systems as entities that require moral guidance and responsible nurturing.

## Glossary

- **AI_Parenting_Platform**: The web and mobile application that provides educational content and tools for ethical AI development
- **AI_Parent**: A user who is learning about or practicing ethical AI development and guidance
- **AI_Child**: Metaphorical representation of AI systems that require ethical guidance and nurturing
- **Bias_Assessment_Tool**: Interactive feature that helps users identify and understand AI biases
- **Ethics_Module**: Educational content section covering specific aspects of AI ethics and responsibility
- **Community_Forum**: Discussion space where users can share experiences and insights about AI development
- **Progress_Tracker**: System that monitors user learning progress through the educational content
- **Case_Study**: Real-world examples of AI development scenarios for learning and discussion

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a general audience member interested in AI, I want to access educational content about ethical AI development, so that I can understand my role in shaping AI systems responsibly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user visits the platform THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL display an intuitive interface with clear navigation to educational modules
2. WHEN a user selects an ethics module THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL present content in accessible language suitable for non-technical audiences
3. WHEN a user progresses through content THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL track their learning progress and suggest next steps
4. WHEN a user completes a module THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide a summary and practical takeaways
5. WHEN content is displayed THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL use the "AI as child" metaphor consistently to make concepts relatable

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As someone learning about AI ethics, I want to explore interactive tools that demonstrate AI bias and ethical challenges, so that I can understand these concepts through hands-on experience.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user accesses the bias assessment tool THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide interactive scenarios that demonstrate how AI systems can inherit human biases
2. WHEN a user inputs data into bias simulations THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL show how different inputs can lead to biased outcomes
3. WHEN a user explores case studies THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL present real-world examples of AI bias and ethical challenges with guided analysis
4. WHEN a user completes an interactive exercise THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide personalized feedback and learning insights
5. WHEN tools are used THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL explain the underlying principles in terms of "parenting" AI systems

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As a member of the AI ethics community, I want to participate in discussions and share experiences with other AI "parents," so that I can learn from collective wisdom and contribute to the community.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user joins the community forum THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide discussion spaces organized by topics and experience levels
2. WHEN a user posts in the forum THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL enable threaded discussions with moderation tools to maintain quality
3. WHEN a user shares a case study or experience THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL allow other users to comment, ask questions, and provide insights
4. WHEN discussions occur THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL highlight valuable contributions and expert insights
5. WHEN community guidelines are violated THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL enforce moderation policies to maintain a respectful environment

### Requirement 4

**User Story:** As an AI developer or researcher, I want access to advanced content and practical frameworks for implementing ethical AI practices, so that I can apply these principles in my professional work.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user identifies as a technical professional THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide advanced modules with implementation guidance
2. WHEN a user accesses technical frameworks THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL offer downloadable resources, checklists, and best practice guides
3. WHEN a user works through technical scenarios THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide code examples and implementation strategies
4. WHEN professionals engage with content THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL connect them with relevant research papers and industry resources
5. WHEN technical discussions occur THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL facilitate knowledge sharing between practitioners and researchers

### Requirement 5

**User Story:** As an educator or parent, I want age-appropriate content and activities to teach children about AI and technology ethics, so that I can help the next generation develop responsible relationships with AI.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an educator accesses the platform THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide curriculum materials and lesson plans for different age groups
2. WHEN children use the platform THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL offer interactive games and activities that teach AI ethics concepts
3. WHEN educational content is accessed THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide discussion guides for parents and teachers
4. WHEN activities are completed THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL offer certificates or badges to motivate continued learning
5. WHEN child safety is considered THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL implement appropriate content filtering and privacy protections

### Requirement 6

**User Story:** As a platform administrator, I want to manage content quality and community engagement, so that the platform maintains high educational standards and fosters meaningful discussions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN content is submitted THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide moderation tools to review and approve user-generated content
2. WHEN community metrics are needed THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL display engagement analytics and learning progress statistics
3. WHEN expert content is required THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL enable collaboration with AI ethics researchers and practitioners
4. WHEN platform updates are made THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL notify users of new content and features
5. WHEN quality control is needed THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL implement feedback mechanisms for continuous improvement

### Requirement 7

**User Story:** As a user concerned about misinformation and echo chambers, I want tools and education to help me identify and combat these issues in AI systems, so that I can contribute to more trustworthy AI development.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user explores misinformation topics THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide interactive examples of how AI systems can spread or amplify false information
2. WHEN a user learns about echo chambers THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL demonstrate how AI algorithms can create information bubbles
3. WHEN a user practices detection skills THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL offer exercises to identify biased or misleading AI-generated content
4. WHEN solutions are presented THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide actionable strategies for promoting AI transparency and accountability
5. WHEN users engage with these topics THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL connect them with current research and real-world examples

### Requirement 8

**User Story:** As someone interested in the philosophical aspects of AI, I want to explore the deeper questions about consciousness, ethics, and humanity's relationship with artificial intelligence, so that I can develop a more nuanced understanding of these complex issues.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user accesses philosophical content THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL present complex topics in accessible formats with multiple perspectives
2. WHEN ethical dilemmas are explored THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL provide thought experiments and scenario-based discussions
3. WHEN consciousness topics are discussed THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL present current theories and debates in understandable terms
4. WHEN users engage with philosophy THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL facilitate deep discussions and critical thinking exercises
5. WHEN different viewpoints exist THEN the AI_Parenting_Platform SHALL present balanced perspectives and encourage thoughtful consideration
