# Dog Angelenos - SEO Optimization Guide

## Goal: Top 3 Ranking for Dog Training in Los Angeles

### Current SEO Features Implemented

1. **Structured Data (Schema.org)**
   - LocalBusiness schema for Google Business Profile
   - Aggregate ratings and reviews
   - Service areas and locations
   - Opening hours and contact info

2. **Meta Tags & SEO**
   - Optimized title tags with location keywords
   - Compelling meta descriptions
   - Open Graph tags for social sharing
   - Proper heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3)

3. **Content Optimization**
   - Location-specific keywords (LA, Los Angeles, West Hollywood, Santa Monica)
   - Service-specific keywords (dog training, puppy training, obedience)
   - Natural keyword placement throughout content
   - Long-tail keywords in descriptions

4. **Technical SEO**
   - Sitemap.xml for search engine crawling
   - Robots.txt for crawler directives
   - Fast loading with Next.js optimization
   - Mobile-responsive design
   - Semantic HTML structure

### Next Steps to Reach Top 3
